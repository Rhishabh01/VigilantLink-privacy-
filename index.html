Privacy Policy for VigilantLink

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

At VigilantLink, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how our browser extension processes information and how that information is used to provide website security analysis.

1. Information We Process

A. URL Analysis Data

When you hover over a link or request a security analysis through VigilantLink, the URL being investigated may be sent to our backend infrastructure for security analysis. This may include:

  • The target URL submitted for analysis.
  • Hostname, path and query information.
  • Redirect destinations identified during analysis.
  • Website-related information required for threat detection.

B. Website Screenshots and Analysis

VigilantLink may use automated website analysis and screenshot processing to examine suspicious website characteristics. Screenshots and related webpage information may be processed by the backend for security assessment.

C. Extension Settings

User preferences, such as theme and enabled or disabled website settings, are stored locally on the device using browser extension storage. These settings are not intentionally uploaded to the VigilantLink backend.

D. Technical Information

The backend may process technical information necessary to handle requests, manage analysis operations, maintain temporary caches and diagnose service errors. Hosting infrastructure may also process technical connection information as part of normal service operation.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by VigilantLink is used for:

  • Detecting potentially malicious and phishing URLs.
  • Checking URL reputation through Google Safe Browsing.
  • Examining website redirects and destinations.
  • Performing screenshot-based website analysis.
  • Generating security scores and threat classifications.
  • Improving analysis reliability and response performance.
  • Maintaining temporary caches to reduce repeated analysis.

VigilantLink does not use submitted URLs or security analysis information for targeted advertising or user profiling.

3. Data Storage and Retention

  • VigilantLink uses temporary caching mechanisms, including Redis, to improve performance and avoid repeatedly analyzing identical URLs.
  • URL-related cache entries and security analysis results may be temporarily retained according to configured cache expiration policies.
  • Pending analysis information may also be temporarily retained while an analysis is being completed.
  • We do not intend to maintain a permanent browsing history through the URL analysis cache.

Backend hosting providers may maintain operational logs or technical records according to their own configurations and retention policies.

Data Protection

Communication between the browser extension and the backend is protected using HTTPS/TLS encryption. We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the service. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

4. Third-Party Services

Google Safe Browsing

VigilantLink uses Google Safe Browsing to check URLs against known unsafe web resources. Information required for these security checks may be transmitted to Google through its security APIs.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Backend Infrastructure

URLs and related analysis information are processed through backend infrastructure to provide security analysis functionality. Infrastructure providers may process technical information necessary to operate their services.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and data-handling practices.

5. Data Sharing

VigilantLink does not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed or shared with third-party services when necessary to provide security analysis, operate backend infrastructure or comply with applicable legal obligations.

VigilantLink does not intentionally share submitted URLs or analysis results with advertisers for advertising purposes.

6. Browser Permissions and User Choices

VigilantLink may request browser permissions necessary to provide its security features, including URL analysis, relevant navigation monitoring and displaying security information.

Users can manage available extension settings or uninstall VigilantLink through their browser's extension management interface.

Uninstalling the extension stops its extension functionality but does not automatically delete information that may already have been processed or retained by backend infrastructure or third-party providers.

7. Children's Privacy

VigilantLink is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children and does not intentionally request such information.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when VigilantLink's features, infrastructure or data-handling practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised Last Updated date.
